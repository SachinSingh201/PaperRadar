About Paper Radar

An ethical, lightweight, automated intelligence pipeline designed for researchers, engineers, and machine learning practitioners.

Architecture & Core Principles

Paper Radar is built around a zero-attack-surface static architecture. It runs on a lightweight schedule, fetches only public feeds and APIs, extracts concise factual summaries, and outputs a static site with no backend databases exposed to the public internet.

  • Official Feeds & APIs First: Only official feeds (Atom/RSS) and public REST APIs are indexed. No paywalled or login-gated content is ever scraped.
  • Robots.txt Enforced: All domains are checked against their official robots.txt. Disallowed paths are completely skipped.
  • Rate Limiting: Enforces a strict 3-second delay between requests per domain to ensure zero server burden on publishers.
  • Untrusted Data Defense: All fetched content is treated as untrusted data and strictly sanitized against prompt injections.
  • Privacy First: Zero trackers, zero analytics, zero external scripts, zero advertising.

Scoring Algorithm

Every paper and release receives a normalized composite score calculated as:

Score = 0.35 × Recency + 0.25 × Source Authority + 0.25 × Topic Relevance + 0.15 × Trending Signals

Recency follows an exponential decay with a 72-hour half-life, ensuring fresh breakthroughs are highlighted while high-impact models remain discoverable.
