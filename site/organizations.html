Different Tasks, Different Timesteps: Temporal Task-Aware Quantization for Diffusion Language Models

- Diffusion Language Models (DLMs) introduce unique quantization challenges due to their iterative denoising process.
- Building on these findings, we introduce T 2Quant, a task and time-aware mixedprecision quantization framework that uses a label-free sensitivity signal to optimally allocate activation precision across denoising steps under a fixed bit-width budget.
